About

SSC is a leading manufacturer, exporter & supplier of water treatment  chemicals and other speciality additives for various industries, operating  Nationwide and Internationally. Our chemicals are used to treat industrial  water in cooling towers, boilers, reverse osmosis plants, ETPs, STPs and  many other speciality applications. We are a R & D based company with an  experience of over 30 years guiding us to develop new solutions for various  application and industries all under one roof.

SSC provides solutions to multiple processes and industries, some of which  include paper & pulp, Textiles, chemical processes, sugar process, water  treatment, lubricating & petroleum industry, etc.,. The array of chemicals  we offer are sourced from trustworthy manufacturers thus ensuring an  unimpeachable range of products are dispatched in the markets nationally  and internationally.

Our Directors Mr. V.Bala Krishnan (Msc in Industrial Chemistry)  & Mr. B.Kapilan (B. Tech in Chemical Engineering) are the key innovators  and technical applications specialist with their vast hands on experience  and in-depth knowledge which they brings into practices. They spearheads  & implies that our committed endeavour is to develop & delivery solutions  to the ever evolving requirements in any process or industry. SSC is built  around robust infrastructure & dedicated process teams, which are well  equipped & trained to maintain technical parameters and to operate on an  optimum level.
